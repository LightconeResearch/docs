On a cluster, the [Quick Start](/quickstart) needs a few changes: where uv and its cache live, how the
Lightcone CLI reaches the batch queues, and how Lightcone Lab gets into the centre's JupyterLab. Each
page below covers them for one centre, in the order to do them.

| Cluster                          | Covers                                                          |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [NERSC](/clusters/nersc)         | Perlmutter: uv, the CLI, your agent, Slurm queues and jupyter.nersc.gov |
| [CC-IN2P3](/clusters/cc-in2p3)   | Under construction                                              |

## Your cluster isn't listed

Follow the [Quick Start](/quickstart), then describe the cluster's queues in
`~/.lightcone/compute.yaml` as [Configuring compute](/lightcone-cli/configuring-compute) explains.
For Lightcone Lab, install it into the Python environment that runs your JupyterLab, as its
[Quick Start](/lightcone-lab/getting-started) shows. On a JupyterHub whose environment you can't
change, install the workbench only into your home directory, as on
[NERSC](/clusters/nersc#add-lightcone-lab-to-jupyternerscgov): most JupyterLabs look there for your own
extensions. Tell us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/EbG6JKuyAx) which cluster you use, and what you
had to change.
