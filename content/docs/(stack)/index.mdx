The Lightcone Research Stack is Lightcone Research's tooling for research analyses described
with [ASTRA](https://astra-spec.org/latest/). You describe an analysis in an `astra.yaml`
specification; the stack runs it, keeps every result tied to the choices that produced it, and
carries it through to a written report.
